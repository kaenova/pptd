# Promotion Design Baseline

Shared requirements for promotion presets. Read this file with exactly one selected preset's `design.md`. That preset's PART B overrides this baseline where they conflict; do not combine PART B sections from different presets.

**Creative commitment**: lead with one memorable creative idea while keeping every claim verifiable. Structure, composition, charts, imagery, and visual narrative must follow content rather than default layouts.

**No AI slop**: avoid templated decoration, unjustified card arrays, equal-split grids, arbitrary gradients or glassmorphism, icon walls, hollow oversized type, repeated skeletons, and unrelated visual gimmicks. Every slide needs clear content-based design judgment.

**Landscape delivery**: final deck uses 16:9 landscape. Recompose portrait or other-ratio source material; do not preserve its canvas ratio.

**Layout coordination**: selected preset governs final visual rules. Per-page layout/visual-task instruction governs structure, primary anchor, and reading order. Follow both.

## Global Prohibitions

1. No default cards: avoid rounded rectangles or rectangular cards for hierarchy/alignment unless requested. Functional information containers are allowed. Prefer rules, whitespace, type scale, and weight.
2. No unjustified equal splits: avoid default thirds, quarters, or 2×2 layouts unless content warrants them.
3. No generic AI palettes: avoid unjustified gradients, neon mixes, rainbow flares, glass cards, and glowing borders.
4. No elements that conflict with selected preset's visual language.
5. Keep chart backgrounds transparent or matching page background, except reversed treatments on dark grounds.
6. Avoid empty planned regions, artificial padding, and cramming; use negative space deliberately.

## Promotion Category Baseline

Brand presentations should land one memorable creative idea per slide while keeping claims auditable. Use a creative-led story, medium density, and deliberate rhythm between evidence-heavy slides and spacious statement slides.

### Content and visual discipline

1. Structure the story around proposition, audience/market evidence, development of big idea, channel/content system, results with measurement context, and next direction.
2. Give a small number of headline metrics visual priority; arrange supporting evidence in compact modules.
3. Use authentic photography or illustration only when suited to the selected preset; never use stock assets just to fill space.
4. Let the selected preset define display and workhorse typefaces. Keep capitalization and tracking consistent; set large numbers in display face.
5. Use one brand color family plus a contrasting accent, following preset background and palette. Avoid rainbow charts.
6. Keep charts focused, use few series and direct labels. Timelines, funnels, and audience bars suit this category. Icons must match preset line/fill language; no multicolor clip art.

### Evidence and structure

1. One big idea per slide; title states campaign proposition or chapter message and every module serves it.
2. Metrics (including impressions, completion, engagement, and conversion) state platform, period, and sample scope. Distinguish outputs from outcomes; give outcomes priority.
3. Timeline slides name each wave/phase and include phase-specific metric when applicable.
4. Tie audience demographics to behavior and value; do not show bare percentages.
5. Case studies use consistent fields: context, action, numerical evidence, implication.
6. Social/UGC evidence states authentic scale and shows representative content structures. Redraw third-party platform screenshots.
7. Every number includes unit, time reference, and source/method context; label estimates as internal/illustrative.
8. End with next direction, budget/allocation, and measurement framework; no thank-you-only close.
9. Imagery and expressive shapes must follow selected preset; use authentic assets, never generic blocks by default.
10. Title read-through test: slide titles alone should tell complete story.

### Density

Follow selected preset's Density Baseline for slide-specific evidence modules, charts, and annotations. No universal numeric target. Keep body copy concise unless selected preset explicitly allows long-form narrative.
